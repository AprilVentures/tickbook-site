    One account, two places

    At the till and online. One balance, one limit, one statement.

    A trade customer who buys at your counter on Monday and on your website on Thursday has one account, not two.

    In your shop, with Shopify POS

    At the till.

    • How they buyYour staff add the customer to the cart and finish the sale with Mark unpaid. It goes on the account.
    • What's checked firstThe till tile shows the balance, what's overdue, the credit limit and the available credit before the sale.
    • Over the limit, overdue or on holdThe tile warns your staff, and they decide. You can also require a manager's approval code.
    • Getting the goodsThey take it with them.
    • Paying it offTake a payment at the till, or record a cheque or bank transfer in the admin.
    On your website, at checkout

    Online.

    • How they buyA signed-in customer with an account chooses Pay on account at checkout. Nothing to pay then.
    • What's checked firstTickbook checks the account and the order against the limit before Pay on account is offered.
    • Over the limit, overdue or on holdBy default they still see it, marked "needs approval" with the reason, and the order waits for you to approve or decline.
    • Getting the goodsClick and collect at any of your locations, or delivery.
    • Paying it offA link on their statement to pay online, once you switch that on.

    Either way, payments settle the oldest orders first, and every account gets a statement each month.

    Straight answers

    What it needs, and what it doesn't do.

    Better you know now than after you've set it up.

    A Shopify online store. Shopify POS is optional: add it if you sell on account at a counter.

    Online, the customer must be signed in and have an account, and the order must be in your shop's currency.

    Works without Shopify B2B, and alongside it if you use both.

    Tickbook doesn't lend money and adds no interest or fees. You run your own accounts, on your own terms.

    It can't stop a sale at the till. Shopify doesn't let apps do that, so the tile warns your staff and anything that needs a look is listed under Exceptions.

    It can't open the cash drawer for an account payment. Shopify doesn't let apps do that either.

    A statement is not a VAT invoice.

    Questions

    The ones shops ask first.

    Does Tickbook lend money or charge interest?

    No. Tickbook doesn't lend money and adds no interest or fees. Your shop runs its own customer accounts, on its own terms. Tickbook keeps the record and does the checking.

    What is Mark unpaid?

    It's Shopify POS's own option to complete a sale without payment. Tickbook puts that sale on the customer's account.

    Why can't a customer see Pay on account?

    They must be signed in and have a Tickbook account, and the order must be in your shop's currency. If you've set a Pay on account tag, they need that tag too.

    What happens when someone is over their limit?

    Online, by default they still see Pay on account, marked "needs approval" with the reason, and the order waits under Exceptions for you to approve or decline. You can choose to hide it in those cases instead. At the till, the tile warns your staff before the sale.

    Do I need Shopify B2B as well?

    No. Shopify B2B gives companies their own prices and payment terms online. Tickbook keeps one account across the till and online, with the limit checked and a monthly statement. They do different jobs and you can use both.

    Can I bring over my existing accounts?

    Yes. Import your customers and what each one owes from a CSV file, or start from the unpaid orders already in your Shopify. Nothing is written until you've checked the totals.

    Does it work with my accounts software?

    It works with Xero and QuickBooks Online. Keep the accounts in Tickbook and send sales and payments across, or keep them in Xero and let Tickbook read each customer's balance.

    Do you do bar tabs?

    Yes, at the till: open a tab, add rounds, and close it paid or on the customer's account.

    What if my subscription lapses?

    Everything works for 14 days with a reminder. After that the till tile pauses, Pay on account is hidden online and emails pause. Nothing recorded is lost.

    What happens if I uninstall?

    Your data is kept for 48 hours in case you reinstall, then deleted, so export your accounts first. Pay on account is a payment method you added in Shopify, so deactivate it there too: once Tickbook is gone, nothing checks who can choose it.
